The map aircraft and vessel vectors in `app/src/main/res/drawable/map_aircraft.xml`
and `map_vessel.xml` contain unmodified path geometry from Google Material Icons
(Apache License 2.0), with native Android vector wrappers and Hrafnsyn colors.

Sources:
- https://github.com/google/material-design-icons/blob/master/src/maps/flight/materialicons/24px.svg
- https://github.com/google/material-design-icons/blob/master/src/maps/directions_boat/materialicons/24px.svg
- https://github.com/google/material-design-icons/blob/master/LICENSE

The launcher artwork shares the iOS raven-eye image; its generated-image provenance
is preserved in `app_icon.png.json` and the PNG metadata.
